A better slider, safer boosting, and Safari support for people who build it themselves.

## What's new

**A slider that follows your ears**
- The slider is now a loudness scale: 0 to 100% fills the first 40% of the track, and above 100% every equal move sounds like the same step louder. Before, the top third of the slider barely changed anything.
- Dragging snaps to 100%, and more gently to 200% and 300%. The level you snapped to lights up under the track. Hold Shift while dragging to move freely.
- Double-click the slider to go back to 100%.
- Keyboard: arrows move 1%, Shift+arrows and Page Up/Down move 10%, Home and End jump to 0% and 500%.
- Trackpad scrolling now moves in sensible steps instead of jumping hundreds of percent in one swipe, and Shift+wheel for 1% steps works on Windows and macOS.
- Screen readers announce the level and sound mode, for example "250 percent, Voice".

**Safer boosting**
- Big jumps up (a preset, unmute, restore) fade in over about a quarter second instead of blasting.
- Above 100%, peaks that would clip are held just under full scale, so loud audio no longer distorts at high levels. At 100% or below the sound is untouched.

**Safari**
- The project now builds for Safari 16.4+. Safari extensions must be wrapped in a macOS app, so there is no download: follow the [Safari steps in the README](https://github.com/cngil/volume-tabs#safari) to build and sign it with your own Xcode. Safari support has not been tested on a Mac yet; please [open an issue](https://github.com/cngil/volume-tabs/issues) if something does not work.

**Smaller fixes**
- More breathing room at the bottom of the popup, and the "Not working? Use tab capture" line moved to a quiet spot at the bottom.
- Controls on pages the browser blocks now clearly look disabled.
- Letter icons use the site's name (S for open.spotify.com instead of O).
- Alignment fixes in the popup and settings.

## Install

**Chrome, Edge, Brave** (`volume-tabs-chrome-1.1.0.zip`)
1. Unzip the file.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on *Developer mode*.
3. Click *Load unpacked* and select the unzipped folder.

**Firefox** (`volume-tabs-firefox-1.1.0.zip`)
1. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`.
2. Click *Load Temporary Add-on* and select the zip file.

The Firefox package is not signed by Mozilla yet, so Firefox removes it on restart, and opening it directly shows "not verified".

## Privacy

Unchanged: no data collection, no network requests, no remote code. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/cngil/volume-tabs/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
